The Technology Behind It

Understanding our advanced verification algorithms

Feature Extraction

Our AI identifies and extracts hundreds of unique features from each signature:

  • Stroke width and consistency
  • Pen pressure variations
  • Writing speed patterns
  • Character proportions and spacing
  • Connection points and loops
  • Baseline and slant angles

Pattern Recognition

Deep learning models compare extracted features against known patterns:

  • Genuine signature characteristics
  • Common forgery techniques
  • Tracing and simulation markers
  • Natural variation vs. reproduction
  • Temporal consistency checks
  • Statistical anomaly detection

Confidence Scoring

Multiple algorithms vote on the final verdict with weighted confidence:

  • Ensemble model voting
  • Uncertainty quantification
  • Cross-validation checks
  • Threshold optimization

Verification Workflow

The system follows a structured workflow to ensure consistent and reliable verification results:

  • Signature image upload and validation
  • Automatic preprocessing and enhancement
  • Feature extraction from the signature image
  • Pattern comparison against stored references
  • Confidence scoring and decision generation
  • Presentation of verification results to the user
